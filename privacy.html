2. Camera and microphone

The app asks your browser for camera and microphone access to show a preview, record and broadcast. Audio and video are processed on your device. Video leaves your device only when you press Go Live, and then only to the destinations you selected (for example YouTube, Facebook, a custom RTMP/WHIP server) or to the streaming gateway configured for that purpose. We do not record or keep your live stream.

3. Google and other services you connect

Sign in with Google gives us your verified email address, name and profile identifier.

YouTube (scope youtube): used only when you choose to prepare and start a live broadcast on your own channel, and to read that broadcast’s status so the app can show whether it is really live.

Google Drive (scope drive.file): used only to upload a recording that you choose to upload, into your own Drive. The app can access only files it created.

Facebook / Meta: used only when you connect a Page to go live on it.

Access tokens from these services are held in your browser’s memory for the current session only and are not saved by us. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and in your Facebook settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy. We use Google user data only to provide the features described above, do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide those features or as required by law, do not use it for advertising, and do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security, or to comply with law.

4. Who else handles data

  • Google (Apps Script, Sheets, Drive) stores the data described in section 1 and processes sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app’s files and may log basic request data under its own policy.
  • Streaming platforms you choose (YouTube, Facebook, others) receive your stream under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Streaming gateway operator, if multi-platform streaming is enabled: the video passes through that server, and the stream keys you enter are held in its memory only while relaying.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Children

The app is not for children under 13 and registration is refused for them. Users aged 13–17 must confirm that a parent or guardian agrees to their use. Parents can contact us to review or delete a minor’s data.

6. Retention and your rights

  • Export: Profile → export your data.
  • Delete: Profile → delete account. This removes your account, profile, preferences, platform connections, stream history, schedules and recording metadata from the Google Sheet.
  • Security/audit log entries record that an account was deleted but not your content. Automatic backup copies of the sheet are kept for a limited time (the 14 most recent) and are then removed, so deleted data can remain in older backups until they rotate out.
  • Recordings on your device or in your Drive are yours; delete them there.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by email.

7. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, signed expiring sessions, request rate limits, account lockout after repeated failures, access control by role, and secret redaction in logs. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password and keep your device updated.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change and significant changes will be announced in the app.
