Preferences

Mobile limitations

Camera access and continuous broadcasting can be interrupted by operating-system restrictions, browser limits, screen locking, battery-saving modes and network changes. GD LIVE STUDIO asks the browser to keep the screen awake while recording or broadcasting where supported, but background streaming is not guaranteed.
